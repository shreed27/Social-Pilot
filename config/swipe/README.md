# Swipe files

Drop viral posts from your niche here as style references, one file per page:
`insta_main.md`, `linkedin_main.md`, `x_main.md`.

Paste the post text (and describe the visuals for carousels/reels), plus why you think it worked.
The writer studies their structure, rhythm and hooks. It never copies them: Instagram down-ranks
pages that repost, and copied posts don't build a following.

Example entry:

    ## Carousel, ~40k shares
    Cover: "Your resume is getting rejected in 6 seconds. Here's why."
    Slides: one mistake per slide, a red ✗ on the bad example and a green ✓ on the fix.
    Why it worked: specific, a little scary, easy to send to a friend job-hunting.
